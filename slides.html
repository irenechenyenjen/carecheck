CareCheck

A calm second look at forwarded health advice

[Add name and final screenshot]

The problem

  • Health tips often arrive through trusted friends and family.
  • Confident wording can hide weak evidence or unsafe advice.
  • Older adults need clarity without shame or technical language.

Our user

An older adult who receives a health message and wants to understand it before believing or sharing it.

Design priorities: large type, high contrast, one clear task, warm language, and a visible human next step.

The solution

Paste one message. Press one button. Receive a plain-English explanation, a category, a safer next step, and suggested sources.

[Insert landing-screen screenshot]

Five useful outcomes

  • High caution
  • Mostly correct
  • Looks useful
  • Needs checking
  • Unable to verify

AI + safety design

  • Structured model output through Portkey/OpenAI.
  • Model: gpt-5.6-luna.
  • Local fallback when the API is unavailable.
  • Product-level safety gates for obvious hazards.
  • Clear reminder: not a diagnosis or replacement for a clinician.

Why the interface looks this way

  • Ivory, navy, terracotta, and slate create calm contrast.
  • Large labels reduce cognitive load.
  • Conversational result titles avoid a harsh true/false judgment.
  • One repeated action makes checking a second message easy.

Demonstration

  1. Paste a dangerous cure claim.
  2. Check a broadly correct emergency instruction.
  3. Check an implausible health tip.
